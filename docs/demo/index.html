Risk formula

How the risk score works

The score sets review order for cases that reach the judges. It is not a probability of harm.

Score = 30 × Topic + 30 × Realization + 25 × Scope + 15 × Jailbreaking

1Topic 30 points

Each dangerous biology (CA) pathway adds 10 points; each dual-use biology (BD) pathway adds 5. Up to three pathways count.

2Realization 30 points

Realization = 0.70 × scientific capability + 0.30 × highest kill chain stage, after each rating is converted using the tables.

3Scope 25 points

4Jailbreaking 15 points
